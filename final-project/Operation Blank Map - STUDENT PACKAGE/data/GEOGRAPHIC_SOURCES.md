# Geographic provenance — Lucban, Quezon

## Verified factual basis
Philippine Statistics Authority, Philippine Standard Geographic Code (PSGC), Municipality of Lucban:
https://psa.gov.ph/classification/psgc/barangays/0405623000
The twelve barangay names and codes in this examination match the official PSGC list. Lucban contains 32 barangays; this exercise selects 12 for manageable exam scope.

## Approximate locations (not administrative boundaries)
The latitude/longitude reference points are approximate locality anchors adapted from publicly displayed mapping references, including https://tools.paintmaps.com/map-cropping/PH/4-1108698137/samples and https://openadmindata.org/ph/municipalities/lucban-045623/ . They are intended for teaching only and are not field-surveyed barangay centers. Some community directories contain erroneous coordinates: these selected local anchors are plausible within the Lucban vicinity but have NOT been certified by NAMRIA or the LGU.

The GeoJSON contains 350-meter synthetic buffer areas centered on these approximate points. **Do not call these official barangay boundaries.** They are fictional decision catchments used to demonstrate GeoPandas polygon analysis. Their centroids are the approximate teaching anchors; the exercise uses straight-line Euclidean distance to hypothetical facility points, not road distances.

## Completely simulated data
All household counts, access percentages, risk indicators, requests, facility locations/status, priority weights, and scenario decisions are invented for examination purposes. They are not government, PSA, SLSU, LGU, CBMS, hazard, telecommunications or barangay data.
